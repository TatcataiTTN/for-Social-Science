Group 2 — School size & ratios (quartile variables)

The four variables with the _Q suffix are all international quartiles (values 1-4, 1=lowest, 4=highest across the entire international PISA sample, not just Vietnam) — this is why they belong to the ordinal scale, not interval/ratio (see Module 01).

Group 3 — Seven school-level WLE (Weighted Likelihood Estimate) indices

What is a WLE? Each index below is NOT a single survey question — it is the combined result of SEVERAL related questions, scaled using a Rasch/IRT (Item Response Theory) measurement model. The principal answers a set of specific questions (e.g. how often a certain behaviour occurs), and PISA's software combines them into ONE score on an internationally standardised scale (mean ≈0, SD ≈1 across the OECD sample). A positive value means above the international average, negative means below — there is no natural unit like "count" or "%". This is an interval scale, not a ratio scale.
VariableMeaningVerification source
EDULEADEducational Leadership index — measures how often the principal engages in instructional-leadership behaviours, built from 7 items (SC201) such as how often the principal "supports co-operation among teachers to develop new teaching practices"✅ Verified via PISA 2022 research documentation (SC201 section of the School Questionnaire)
NEGSCLIMNegative School Climate index — the principal's own rating of how much discipline/learning-hindering-behaviour problems occur at the school✅ Verified: "Index of negative school climate", a principal-reported index
STAFFSHORTStaff Shortage index — built from 4 items: lack of teaching staff, inadequate/poorly qualified teaching staff, lack of assisting staff, inadequate/poorly qualified assisting staff✅ Verified via official PISA 2022 technical documentation
EDUSHORTEducational Material Shortage index — built from 4 items: lack of educational material, inadequate/poor-quality educational material, lack of physical infrastructure, inadequate/poor-quality physical infrastructure✅ Verified via official PISA 2022 technical documentation
DIGPREPPreparedness for Digital Learning index — measures how well the school has prepared its infrastructure/processes for online/digital teaching✅ Full name verified via PISA 2022 research, shown to relate to EDULEAD (better leadership → better digital preparedness)
AVLRSOFTAvailability of Learning Software index✅ Confirmed directly from the variable label stored in the .sav file: "Availability of learning software (WLE)"
ENCOURPGSchool Encouragement of Parent or Guardian Involvement index✅ Confirmed directly from the variable label stored in the .sav file: "School encouragement of parent or guardian involvement (WLE)"

The remaining 5 variables now have confirmed official labels from the .sav file: ABGSCIE="Ability grouping for science classes", PROATCE="Proportion of fully certified teachers", SCHSEL="School selectivity", STDTEST="Use of standardised tests (WLE)", TDTEST="Use of teacher-developed tests (WLE)" — still NOT used in any example/analysis on this site since their detailed value scales haven't been verified, but their meaning is now clear.
