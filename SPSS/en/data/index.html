Datasets

Two datasets are used throughout the course. Every figure in the slides is computed directly from these files with PSPP/Python — no number is hand-typed.

Simulated Classroom — 240 Students

Synthetic data (fixed random seed) simulating a classroom, used for basic t-test/ANOVA/regression/EFA exercises. A few deliberate “traps” (outliers, missing values) are built in for data-cleaning practice.

📄 CSV📓 Jupyter Notebook

PISA 2025 — 195 Vietnamese Schools

REAL data from the PISA 2025 survey (OECD), filtered and aggregated for Vietnam at school level (195 schools). Used for descriptive, t-test, ANOVA, regression and PCA lessons with real rather than simulated data.

💾 SPSS/PSPP data (.sav)📄 CSV📝 PSPP syntax (.sps)
